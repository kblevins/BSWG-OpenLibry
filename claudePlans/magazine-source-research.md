# Magazine Cover Image & Table-of-Contents Sourcing Research

Research into whether cover images and table-of-contents (TOC) text can be
programmatically retrieved for each magazine series in the collection.
Feeds into [magazine-support.md](magazine-support.md) (Step 3/4: bulk import
of TOC text and cover images).

Assumption per series: holdings from the beginning of the series (or 1976)
to present.

**Status as of 2026-07-08:** PieceWork, Spin-Off, Ply, Handwoven, WEFT
Magazine, The Weaver's Journal, Threads (#1–169), and Ornament
(Vol. 13.4+) are all **done** — covers and TOC/full-text acquired and
verified in `scratch/magazine-scrape-inventory.csv`. Threads also has a
separate set of 1008 TOC page images (`scratch/toc-images/threads/`) for
visual lookup, since its full-issue OCR text alone wasn't reliable enough
as a stand-in table of contents on every issue. See
[magazine-data-acquisition-plan.md](magazine-data-acquisition-plan.md)
for the execution-side status (scripts, row counts, tooling installed).
Everything else below is still research-only or blocked on guild input.

## Per-title detail

### PieceWork
- **Status: done.** 191 rows in `scratch/magazine-scrape-inventory.csv`, covers + TOC matched and verified.
- **Publisher/status:** Interweave Press → Long Thread Media (2019 acquisition). Still actively publishing.
- **Cover images:** DiscountMags (discountmags.com/magazine/piecework-digital/back-issues) lists 182 issues 1993–present, each with a cover thumbnail at a predictable URL pattern. Free to view thumbnails; full issue paywalled ($9.99/issue). Zinio also has issue-level pages, subscriber-gated.
- **TOC/index text:** Free downloadable PDF indexes at pieceworkmagazine.com/piecework-indexes/ — Subject Index (Mar 1993–Jan 2015), Author Index (same range), Combined Author/Subject Index (Jan/Feb 2015–Winter 2025). Real text, not scans, covering the entire back-catalog, organized by subject/author rather than per-issue TOC. Ravelry has per-issue source pages (e.g. ravelry.com/patterns/sources/piecework-summer-2021) listing patterns/projects as text, full detail needs free Ravelry login.
- **Access notes:** Cover thumbnails and PDF indexes freely accessible. Full-issue content paywalled. Respect ToS/robots and rate limits on DiscountMags/Zinio/Ravelry before automating at volume.
- **Sources:** pieceworkmagazine.com, pieceworkmagazine.com/piecework-indexes/, discountmags.com/magazine/piecework-digital/back-issues, ravelry.com/patterns/sources/piecework-summer-2021

### Spin-Off

- **Publisher/status:** Long Thread Media. Active (issues confirmed through Summer 2026).
- **Cover images:** shop.longthreadmedia.com/collections/spin-off-magazines has ~222 product pages with predictable URLs (`/products/spin-off-[season]-[year]`) but reliably covers only roughly 2023–2026; older issues sold as bundled "Collection Download" products (e.g. "Spin-Off 1991-1992 Collection," $24.99/8 issues), so cover scraping needs per-collection handling for older decades. - **Access notes:** Cover images and index PDF freely accessible, no login observed. Full back-issue content paywalled (individual purchase or $99.99/yr Digital All Access), scanned images not text — bulk text extraction of full content would need OCR.
- **Sources:** spinoffmagazine.com/library/, spinoffmagazine.com/spin-off-index-2011-2020/, shop.longthreadmedia.com/collections/spin-off-magazines, ravelry.com/patterns/sources/spin-off-magazine-website

### Shuttle, Spindle & Dyepot
- **Publisher/status:** Handweavers Guild of America (HGA), handweavers.org / weavespindye.org. Still actively published, quarterly, not merged/renamed.
- **Cover images:** Not freely available in bulk. Issues live on a third-party "digital edition" flip-book platform (digital.watkinsprinting.com), members-only, JS-rendered/login-walled — automated fetches returned empty content. Digital archive (2007+) gated behind HGA membership login.
- **TOC/index text:** The Watkins viewer advertises direct links from a contents page to articles and full-text search within an issue, implying real text exists inside the paywalled viewer. No consolidated public index found; HGA has historically sought a volunteer to build a cumulative index. No WorldCat article-level index, no fan-maintained index.
- **Access notes:** Everything of value (2007–present) sits behind HGA member login; pre-2007 (1976–2006) has no identified digital source at all. Automated bulk retrieval would require authenticated scraping of a third-party vendor's platform — likely against HGA/Watkins ToS.
- **Verdict:** Not available for bulk programmatic retrieval. Realistic path: manual/OCR digitization of the library's own physical holdings, or a direct permission-based request to HGA for a bulk export.
- **Sources:** weavespindye.org/ssd/, digital.watkinsprinting.com, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handweavers_Guild_of_America, librarything.com/nseries/28773/Shuttle-Spindle-Dyepot

### American Craft
- **Publisher/status:** American Craft Council (ACC), est. 1941 as *Craft Horizons*, renamed 1979. Print edition discontinued as of Winter 2026 (moved to digital-only on craftcouncil.org). ACC itself remains active.
- **Cover images:** ACC Library Digital Collections at digital.craftcouncil.org (CONTENTdm-style, collection p15785coll2) digitized the entire *Craft Horizons* run (1941–1979) plus *American Craft* through 2011 (2024 push added 1991–2011). **2012–2017 (the library's actual holdings) does not appear to be in this digitized archive** — only reachable via ACC's paywalled "Past Issues"/purchase pages, not built for bulk access.
- **TOC/article text:** No confirmed full-text-searchable index; digital.craftcouncil.org returned 403 to automated fetch (bot-blocking). No JSTOR/Internet Archive/HathiTrust presence found.
- **Access notes:** ACC Terms of Use explicitly prohibit reproduction, mass downloading, storing, or redistributing site material.
- **Verdict:** Possible with scraping/OCR only for 1999–2011 (pending ToS permission); 2012–2017 not evidently available at all. Recommend contacting ACC's library/archives directly for a bulk export or licensing arrangement.
- **Sources:** digital.craftcouncil.org, craftcouncil.org/library/archives, craftcouncil.org/articles/more-issues-of-american-craft-now-available-online, craftcouncil.org/terms-of-use, onlinebooks.library.upenn.edu/webbin/serial?id=americancraft

### Fiber Arts
- **Publisher/status:** Founded 1975 by Rob Pulleyn/Kate Mathews (Fiberarts Inc./Altamont Press), first issue Jan/Feb 1976, Asheville NC from 1979. Acquired by Interweave Press. Ceased with the **Summer 2011** issue — Interweave cited insufficient financial support; no acquirer took over the title. (Interweave itself was later acquired by F+W Media in Aug 2012, after Fiberarts had already shut down.) Editorial archive (correspondence, artist statements, production files 1970–2001) donated to Colorado State University's Morgan Library Archives & Special Collections.
- **Cover images / TOC:** No digitization found anywhere —
  - **Internet Archive:** No `sim_fiberarts` or `pub_fiberarts` collection exists; advanced-search API returns 49 items for "fiberarts"/"fiber arts", none are magazine issues (all books/anthologies/guild videos). Zero issue-level coverage.
  - **HathiTrust:** No digitized issues found; catalog/Bibliographic API queries return no records for this title's OCLC number (3301202).
  - **Google Books:** No evidence of scanned magazine issues (only unrelated books with "fiber arts" in the title).
  - **WorldCat:** Standard serial bibliographic record only (OCLC #3301202) — title/publisher/subject headings/library holdings, zero article-level indexing.
  - **Library databases (Art Full Text, Art Index, ARTbibliographies Modern):** No confirmed coverage found.
  - **Fan/community indexes:** None found.
- **Access/legal notes:** Still under U.S. copyright (post-1978 serial; rights likely held by Thrums LLC/Long Thread Media as Interweave's successor). Archive.org and HathiTrust both restrict bulk/automated download of in-copyright material even where scans exist (neither has any here regardless). No clean bulk-download path exists.
- **Verdict:** Not available online in any form. Only path: your own physical scans (covers + TOC pages), or a direct licensing conversation with Long Thread Media/Thrums LLC. Facts (titles, issue TOC entries) aren't copyrightable if you manually transcribe them, but scraping cover art/full text would need permission.
- **Sources:** archives.colostate.edu/repositories/2/resources/163, source.colostate.edu/fiberarts-editorial-archives-donated-morgan-library, fiberfocus.blogspot.com/2011/06/fiberarts-magazine-bites-dust-call-to.html, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F+W, fiberartnow.net (unofficial spiritual successor, separate publication), worldcat.org/title/fiberarts/oclc/3301202

### The Weaver's Journal
- **Publisher/status:** Defunct fiber/weaving quarterly, published 1976–1987 (Boulder, CO area; ran later than earlier research assumed — the final issue is explicitly labeled #46, Fall 1987), later succeeded in spirit by *Weavers* magazine. Confirmed as a separate holding from WEFT Magazine above, not an alternative identification of the same catalog entry.
- **Cover images:** **Done — covers acquired 2026-07-08.** Verified directly against the archive's index page (www2.cs.arizona.edu/patterns/weaving/wj.html): it only hosts issues **#32–#46 (Vol. VIII No.4, Spring 1984, through Vol. XII No.2, Fall 1987)** — 15 issues, not the full 46-issue/1976–1986 run assumed in the original research pass. Issues #1–31 (1976–1983) are not on this archive in any form. Each PDF (`.../periodicals/wj_<n>.pdf`, confirmed URL pattern, 5–8MB each) is a straight image scan (confirmed: `page.get_text()` returns empty on every page — no text layer at all). Page 1 of each PDF is the cover; extracted via `scripts/scrape/extract-weavers-journal-pdfs.py` (PyMuPDF, 200dpi PNG) into `scratch/covers/weavers-journal/wj-<n>.png` — all 15/15 succeeded, spot-checked and clean (Issue 32 cover verified visually: title masthead, volume/issue/date printed cleanly, full-page product photo).
- **TOC/index text:** **Done — TOC OCR'd 2026-07-08.** Same 15 PDFs have zero embedded text (confirmed empty `page.get_text()`), but visual inspection of both the first (#32) and last (#46) issue — spanning both TOC layouts used across the run — confirmed page index 2 (the 3rd page of every PDF) is reliably the full table-of-contents page. Installed tesseract via Homebrew (`brew install tesseract`, this machine already had Homebrew at `/opt/homebrew/bin/brew`, just not on PATH) and rendered that page at 300dpi, then OCR'd it with `scripts/scrape/ocr-weavers-journal-toc.py`. 15/15 succeeded (193–565 words each); spot-checked against the source images and quality is high — article titles/authors are all correctly readable, with only minor character-level noise (e.g. "ff"/"tt" ligature confusion), well above the "good enough for search" bar. A "Ten Year Cumulative Index 1976–1986" also exists commercially via Camilla Valley Farm Weavers' Supply (camillavalleyfarm.com/books/weaversjournal.htm) but is no longer needed now that per-issue OCR succeeded — not pursued.
- **Access notes:** The Arizona archive is freely and publicly downloadable, no login, straightforwardly scriptable given the predictable PDF filename pattern. The title is still in copyright, with unclear reuse rights — the university archive hosts scans with some apparent permission but doesn't state a reuse license for downstream bulk use.
- **Verdict:** Covers and TOC text both done for all 15 issues the archive actually holds (#32–46). Issues #1–31, if the guild holds physical copies of them, have no known digital source and would need physical scanning.
- **Sources:** www2.cs.arizona.edu/patterns/weaving/wj.html, www2.cs.arizona.edu/patterns/weaving/periodicals/wj_32.pdf through wj_46.pdf

### "Weaver's" (XRX Inc.) — likely a second, distinct title mixed into the guild's "Weaver's Journal" holdings
- **Why this entry exists:** The guild's catalog shows a "Weaver's Journal" run spanning **1982–1995** — longer than *The Weaver's Journal* above ever existed (it ended Fall 1987, issue #46). The gap is almost certainly explained by a second, separately-published magazine with a near-identical name shelved/cataloged together with the first.
- **Publisher/status:** *Weaver's* (also seen as "Weaver's Magazine"), published by XRX Inc., edited by Madelyn van der Hoogt, **1986–1999**. Ended when van der Hoogt moved to edit *Handwoven* in 1999; content was later anthologized in XRX's "Best of Weaver's" book series (e.g. *Huck Lace*, *Magic of Doubleweave*, *Overshot is Hot!*). Confirmed as a real, separate quarterly (not just an alternate name for *The Weaver's Journal*) via multiple back-issue listings (e.g. Issue 8, 1st Quarter 1990; Issue 11, 4th Quarter 1990).
- **Overlap with the guild's holdings:** The 1982–1987 portion of the guild's run is very likely *The Weaver's Journal* (Colorado Fiber Center); the 1987/88–1995 portion is very likely this separate *Weaver's* (XRX) title, not a continuation of the same magazine.
- **Confirmation needed:** Pull a few physical issues from the 1988–1995 range and check the masthead/publisher line — "Colorado Fiber Center" vs. "XRX Inc." (or an editor credit to Madelyn van der Hoogt) confirms the split immediately.
- **Cover images / TOC text:** Not yet researched — this is a new title, distinct from the *The Weaver's Journal* sourcing work already done above. No scraping/digitization investigation has been done for *Weaver's* (XRX) yet.
- **Verdict:** Pending physical confirmation from the guild; once confirmed, needs its own sourcing research pass (separate from *The Weaver's Journal*, which is already complete).
- **Sources:** camillavalleyfarm.com/books/weaversjournal.htm, en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madelyn_van_der_Hoogt, amazon.com (Weaver's Magazine back-issue listings, Issues 8 & 11, 1990)

### Women in the Arts

Fiber/textile guild libraries do sometimes carry general women's-art-history titles alongside craft-specific ones for their coverage of women artists broadly, which fits better than an unrelated small feminist-press art zine — no other publication with this exact title turned up. Older/pre-2023 issues push readers to "Become a Member to Read the Latest Issues" — full content is membership-gated. No JSTOR, HathiTrust, or WorldCat article-level index found for this specific title. NMWA's own Betty Boyd Dettre Library and Research Center offers on-site JSTOR/Oxford Art Online access to visitors, but that isn't evidence this title itself is indexed anywhere.
- **Access notes:** nmwa.org's magazine page is publicly browsable for recent covers/blurbs, but full issue access (and any real TOC) requires an NMWA membership login. No bulk/API access apparent.
- **Verdict:** Not really available programmatically beyond the last ~5 years of cover thumbnails; no free TOC text found for any era. Recommend confirming the identification against a physical issue first, then contacting NMWA's library/publications team directly about back-issue access if deeper coverage than 2021-present is wanted.

### Threads

## Key takeaways

1. **Ply, PieceWork, Spin-Off, Handwoven, and now WEFT Magazine are genuinely easy** — free, real text, no OCR, low legal risk for metadata-only use (titles/authors/subjects, not full article text or paywalled cover art). Covers/TOC acquisition for these five is complete.
2. **Fiber Arts is a dead end online** — nothing digitized is publicly reachable. If the guild holds physical copies, scanning those is the only realistic path. **Shuttle, Spindle & Dyepot is deferred, not dead** — the guild plans to request a bulk export directly from HGA, so no scraping/photography effort should go here until that request plays out.
3. **American Craft's 2012–2017 range (the library's actual holdings) isn't covered** by ACC's own digitization effort, which stops at 2011.
4. **"Interweave" is still an ambiguous catalog entry** — feasibility swings wildly depending on which specific publication is meant; check the physical issues before investing engineering effort. **"Weft" is no longer ambiguous** — the guild confirmed it holds both WEFT Magazine (active, easy scrape) and The Weaver's Journal (defunct, scanned-PDF extraction) as two separate titles; both now have their own entries above.
5. **Threads has an unusually strong free source** — Internet Archive already holds fully-OCR'd early issues, no scraping-then-OCR pipeline needed at all for that range.
6. **Women in the Arts (likely NMWA's magazine) is thin online** — only ~5 years of cover thumbnails, no real TOC found anywhere; would need direct outreach to NMWA or physical scanning for older holdings.
7. **Copyright/ToS applies throughout.** Most publishers' full issue content is paywalled and copyrighted; free indexes are metadata (titles/authors/subjects) and safe to use for cataloging. Bulk-scraping paywalled cover art or full article text risks ToS/copyright issues. For membership-gated sources (SDA, NMWA), asking the organization directly for a bulk export is both safer and more likely to succeed than scraping.
